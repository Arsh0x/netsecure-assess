# Ethical-use policy

NetSecure Assess is for systems you own or have explicit written permission to
assess. Before every scan, users must record authorization, scope and purpose.

The application does not include exploitation, password attacks, malware,
persistence, evasion, spoofing, denial-of-service testing or data exfiltration.
Findings are indicators and require human validation. Stop a scan immediately if
scope, ownership or operational impact is uncertain. Follow institutional policy,
applicable law and data-retention requirements.
